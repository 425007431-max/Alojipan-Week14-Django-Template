About the Portal

The NTC BSIT Student Portal is designed to give students a fast, organized way to check course updates, schedules, and department resources without clutter.

Intended Users

Built primarily for Bachelor of Science in Information Technology students, along with instructors and department coordinators sharing academic updates.

Accessibility Feature Implemented

High-Contrast Focus & Keyboard Navigation: All navigation links use semantic <nav> and <a> elements with visible keyboard focus outlines (:focus-visible) and high-contrast text colors over light and dark surfaces.
